GenAI Use & Student Outcomes

50,000 students · pre vs post-semester GPA, skill retention, anxiety & burnout

Key findings (all 50,000 students)

  • Moderate use wins. GPA gain peaks at 5–15 h/week (+0.23) and shrinks to +0.13 at 30–40 h.
  • Heavy use erodes skills. Retention falls to 65.7 at 30–40 h vs 77.1 at 5–10 h.
  • Burnout climbs steeply. 90% of 30–40 h users are high-risk vs 9% at 0–2 h.
  • How you prompt matters. Advanced prompters retain 82.1 vs 71.1 for beginners, at similar hours.
  • Use case matters. Debugging: +0.25 GPA, 78.1 retention. Direct answer generation: +0.13, 73.7.
  • Strict bans backfire on stress. Exam anxiety 4.9 vs ~4.1 elsewhere; high burnout 29.8% vs 23.8%.
  • Freshmen gain least (+0.13) vs Seniors (+0.24) and Graduates (+0.25).
